# Data selection and retained evidence

The final study uses the EPA-hosted Dhaka embassy daily record for 2016–2021 and newly acquired NASA POWER meteorology. The hourly AirNow mirror supports a partial-period station-day check; it is the same monitoring network, not an additional independent site. Sources and uncertainty are described in the README and numerical provenance manifest.

## Findings retained from the earlier work

The observed-record coverage, annual and monthly concentrations, seasonal contrast, coverage-screened trend, weather associations, temporal prediction baselines and paired weather-increment intervals remain the core results. Daily coverage, percentile-defined episodes and predictor-redundancy diagnostics are included to make their scope easier to assess. Both historical evaluation periods are reported.

The original 2000–2025 archive is a separate input, attributed to Paul, Tapon (2026), Mendeley Data V2, DOI [10.17632/9j447cynb9.2](https://doi.org/10.17632/9j447cynb9.2). A matching deposited-file identity does not establish how its underlying concentrations were acquired or generated.

## Results excluded from the final study

The legacy 2000–2025 atmospheric trend, apparent 2022 pollution collapse, causal climate/source attribution, pollutant PCA and model rankings are excluded. The original climate/satellite extraction was not recovered; whole-record feature construction and the changing pollutant source prevent these outputs from supporting the final claims. The smaller reconstructed monthly model experiment is a computational diagnostic rather than a validated environmental forecast.

The additional released-data component uses the exact published reanalysis release and source extracts with explicit credit. Its reproduction results and observed-reference comparisons are separated under `results/reference/` from the primary embassy/NASA-weather results. Original gridded NetCDF acquisition and conversion have not been independently repeated. This extension does not authenticate the older 2000–2025 archive.

Original uploads and full intermediate runs remain separate from the published results. Reproduction of the final study uses only the documented observed-record inputs; see [REPRODUCE.md](REPRODUCE.md).
